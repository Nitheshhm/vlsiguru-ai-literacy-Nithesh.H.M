## Notes

### What did the AI get right?

The AI assistants provided mostly correct explanations for the claims checked during Week 1. The Q4 experiment showed that both ChatGPT and Claude correctly explained the behavior of `list.sort()` and `sorted()`.

### What did it get wrong or leave unsupported?

The Q4 experiment did not expose an incorrect claim. However, an AI answer can still require verification because a fluent response is not automatically proof. Some claims about commercial systems also require checking public documentation rather than assuming that a feature uses AI.

### What did I learn about verification?

I learned that verification should be based on appropriate evidence rather than only on how confident or detailed an AI answer sounds. For technical claims, I should check reliable documentation, standards, or experiments and record the result so another person can inspect the reasoning.
