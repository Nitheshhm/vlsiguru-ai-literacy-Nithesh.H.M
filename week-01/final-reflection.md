# Final Reflection — Week 01

## 1. What Was My Most Useful Learning?
My most useful learning was understanding how AI, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI are related. I also learned that AI-generated answers should be verified before relying on them.

## 2. What Did I Initially Misunderstand?
Initially, I thought that every system that performs an intelligent-looking task must use AI. I learned that simple rule-based programs can also perform useful tasks without machine learning.

## 3. How Did AI Help Me?
AI helped me understand technical concepts, organise my ideas, and explore how AI systems work. It also helped me understand how AI could assist with timing analysis and debugging in VLSI Physical Design.

## 4. What Did I Verify or Correct?
I checked explanations about how LLMs generate text and the difference between RAM and storage using external sources. I also reviewed the relationship between AI, ML, DL, and GenAI to avoid presenting a simplified diagram as a universal rule.

## 5. What Question Do I Still Have for Saturday?
